The Kanban board was used to organize the work by sprint and track tasks from idea to implementation. The main groups were:

- Core finance flows.
- Python implementation and testing.
- Budget alerts and monthly review.
- AI Advisor and AI Coach.
- Final presentation and documentation.

The final version in `main` reflects the completed product work selected for the course delivery.
